Scenario: PBX calls out via a SIP trunk. Carrier proxy challenges with
407 Proxy Authentication Required; PBX re-sends INVITE with Proxy-Authorization
header containing digest credentials; call proceeds to 200 OK.
Common when a SIP trunk has authenticated egress enabled (digest), as opposed
to IP-whitelist trunks.
